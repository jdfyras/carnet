# Rules learned

The lesson, not the fault. Entries of the error journal are private; what a family taught is
written here and shared.

A rule enters this file when its family has been seen twice and the user agrees. When a family
comes back after its rule, do not rewrite the sentence more firmly: change its form. Give it a tool
that produces the value, a required field in a template, a check at commit time, or a hook, and
record that form with `carnet erreur regle <family> --forme "<what now enforces it>"`.
